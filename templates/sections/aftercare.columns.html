{{! aftercare.columns — split by subject. USED BY editorial AND classic. New classes need rules in the SHARED block. Was: one 90-word paragraph covering hair, beard and post-shave care with no structure that said so, followed by two short columns of standing advice. The section had no shape because the content had three subjects and one paragraph. `homepage.aftercare_items` is a NEW OPTIONAL config key (see the patch note in the deliverable README). When it is present the subjects render as columns; when it is absent this falls back to the existing prose blob, so every client config that predates the key keeps working unchanged. It has to be config rather than markup: the subjects a client aftercares for are specific to their trade. One trade's set is meaningless for the next, and a template that names them has a business fact welded into it — which the grep in assert-invariants.mjs would reject, correctly. The two standing notes moved BELOW the subjects into `care__notes` and dropped to the micro tier. They are a footnote to the aftercare, not a fourth and fifth subject, and at the same weight as the subjects they were competing with them. HEAD_ALIGN is not interpolated: both variants that use this template want a left-aligned head, and `classic` neutralises the centre modifier anyway. }}
