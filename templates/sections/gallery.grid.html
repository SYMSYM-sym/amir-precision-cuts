{{! gallery.grid — a full-bleed interstitial band, then the grid. USED BY ALL FOUR VARIANTS. Every class added here needs its rule in the SHARED block of styles.css, above `#if IS_EDITORIAL`. The band is the page's one moment of pause: one image, one line, edge to edge, no section padding. Seven equally-padded sections in a row is what made the old page feel like a form to scroll rather than a place to look at. It is the LEAD gallery image promoted out of the grid — ``#if @first`` for the band, ``#unless @first`` for the tiles — which is why it needs no new asset, no new section id in ALL_SECTIONS, and no change to site-render.mjs. It also inherits the gallery section's own condition for free: a business that supplies no photographs gets no band, because it gets no gallery. The tiles went from 4:3 landscape to 4:5 portrait. The artwork is silhouettes and profiles; a landscape crop of a vertical subject spends most of its area on background, which is why six tiles of near-black read as six empty rectangles. HEAD_ALIGN is still interpolated here — unlike services.columns — because the `gallery` variant centres its section heads by design and this is its title section. }}
