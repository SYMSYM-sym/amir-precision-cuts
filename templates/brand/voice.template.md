## Sentence rules

- One idea per sentence. Cut adverbs.

- Concrete over abstract ("a 7-minute drive from {{landmark}}", not "conveniently located").

## Things we NEVER do
- Emojis. Exclamation points (beyond a very rare emphatic ending).
- Superlatives without evidence ("best", "most", "ultimate").
- Medical claims ("cures", "heals", "treats"). Guarantees about results or pain.
- Coy euphemisms — use plain anatomical or service terms.
- Stock openings ("In today's fast-paced world…", "Let's dive in…").
- Hype verbs ("game-changer", "elevate", "revolutionize", "level up").
- Listicles with rhyming headers.
