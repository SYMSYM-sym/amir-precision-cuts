# THE ORIGINALITY TRAP — this is the part that usually fails
Hyperlocal topics are near-clones of each other. "[service] near A" and "[service] near B"
produce ~0.85+ similar articles and get quarantined forever. So:
1. Cap the hyperlocal bucket at 15 of the 50 topics. Spread the rest across the other buckets.
2. Every near-neighbour topic needs a genuinely DIFFERENT REASON TO EXIST in `notes` — not a
   different noun. "Local guide for [neighborhood]" is a failed note. State explicitly what
   framing the article must NOT reuse.
3. Put the strongest, most distinct topics FIRST — the queue publishes in order.
